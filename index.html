Pilot note

AxoTouch is an experimental project currently in development. Participation in any future pilot will be voluntary and intended for adults 18+.

The experiment explores new ways for people to interact while controlling how much information about themselves they choose to reveal. Identity, appearance, voice, and other personal details may remain private unless a participant chooses otherwise.

Participants will be expected to respect each other's boundaries and consent. Harassment, threats, coercion, non-consensual sexual behavior, or attempts to identify another participant without permission will not be acceptable. You will be able to leave an interaction at any time.

AxoTouch makes no promises about compatibility, relationships, outcomes, or what you may experience. No guarantees. Just an experiment between humans.
